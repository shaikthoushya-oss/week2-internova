About the club

Started by six friends in 2019, Trailwise now has over 300 members. Every hike has a trained leader, a sweep walker at the back, and a first-aid kit in the lead pack.

No hike is longer than 12 km, and nobody is left behind.

Why people join

Easy pace

Groups move at the speed of the slowest walker, with a rest stop every hour.

Small groups

Maximum 15 people per hike, so you actually get to know who you're walking with.

Gear help

New to hiking? Borrow poles and daypacks from the club shelf for your first three trips.
